Need a hand? ↗
Do I need a physical PS4?+

Yes. Noctra is a Windows launcher and setup companion for chiaki-ng. It streams from a real PS4 using the console’s built-in Remote Play service; it is not an emulator.

Is Noctra free?+

Yes. Noctra Link is free and open source under the MIT license. The separately downloaded chiaki-ng engine retains its own upstream license.

Do I need internet to play?+

You need internet for the initial launcher and engine downloads. Local streaming uses your own network, a pairable console account, and its actual Account-ID. Optional PSN features in chiaki-ng may need internet.

What if my console isn’t discovered?+

Keep both devices on the same network and avoid an isolated guest network. Allow chiaki-ng through Windows Firewall on private networks, or register manually using your console’s local IP address.

What affects picture quality and latency?+

Your console, network, and client settings all matter. Ethernet, 720p, and H.264 are a useful starting point. Some protected content and restricted screens may be blocked by the PS4.
